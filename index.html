How it all began

Our Love Story

January 29, 2023

First We Met

They first met at Kaye's wedding, Ella's sister. During the celebration, Ella couldn't help but notice Raymond glancing her way more than once. By the end of the evening, they finally struck up a conversation, and before the night was over, they had already planned a date for the next morning.

January 30, 2023

First Date

Yes, it really happened that fast. What began as a simple plan quickly turned into something meaningful. From that very first date, there was an undeniable connection, and their story began to unfold.

February 03, 2023

In a Relationship

Just six days after their first date, Raymond asked Ella to be his. Though naturally shy, he didn't hesitate when it came to her, and that made the moment even more special.

March 09, 2026

We're Engaged

After three beautiful years together, Raymond proposed during their first international trip. Ella had no idea it was coming, making the moment even more unforgettable and full of surprise.
